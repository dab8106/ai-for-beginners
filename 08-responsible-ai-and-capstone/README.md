## Capstone project

[Lecture 38: AI Careers: Build a Portfolio That Gets Noticed](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mPjLlKMAITw) ends the lessons with your capstone: an AI assistant for a real problem, on one of two tracks. No-code, with a custom assistant and an automation. Or Python, with RAG, tools or an agent. Both count.

- [Capstone brief](capstone/brief.md): the tracks, requirements, steps and a checklist
- [Project plan template](capstone/project-plan-template.md): one page, with your test questions
