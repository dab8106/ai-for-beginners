# Leave Policy: Mumbai Shop Pvt. Ltd. (fictional, for training only)

Effective 1 April 2026.

1. **Annual leave.** Every full-time employee receives 21 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, earned at 1.75 days per completed month of service.
2. **Carry-over.** Up to 10 days of unused annual leave can be carried over to the next calendar year. Any days above 10 lapse on 31 December.
3. **Sick leave.** Employees receive 8 days of paid sick leave per year. A doctor's certificate is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days.
4. **Maternity leave.** Eligible employees receive 26 weeks of paid maternity leave, as per applicable law.
5. **Paternity leave.** Employees receive 10 working days of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 3 months of the child's birth or adoption.
6. **Public holidays.** The company observes 10 public holidays each year. The list is published every December for the following year.
7. **Applying for leave.** Leave must be requested through the HR portal at least 7 days in advance, except for sick leave.
