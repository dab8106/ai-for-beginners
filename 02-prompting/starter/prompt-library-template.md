# My prompt library

Copy this file into Google Docs, Notion, OneNote or any notes app you use every day. Keep one entry per template, using the layout below.

**How to use a template:** copy the prompt, replace everything in `[SQUARE BRACKETS]`, delete any line you don't need, and send it. Never put passwords, bank details or real client data into the brackets. Use "client A" or "amount X" and fill in the real details yourself, outside the AI tool.

---

## Template layout

**Name:** a short name you'll recognise in a search
**Use it when:** one line
**Technique:** four parts / examples / step by step / table or JSON / ask me first
**Tested in:** Claude, ChatGPT, or both · **Last tested:** date
**Prompt:**

```
[the prompt, with placeholders in square brackets]
```

**Notes:** what to watch for in the answer, and the follow-up that usually helps

## Worked example

**Name:** Polite payment reminder
**Use it when:** a client's payment is late and the relationship matters
**Technique:** four parts
**Tested in:** both · **Last tested:** [DATE]
**Prompt:**

```
You are [MY ROLE] at [COMPANY TYPE]. You write warm, clear emails that keep
good relationships with clients.

Context: [CLIENT A]'s payment for invoice [INVOICE NUMBER], dated
[INVOICE DATE], is [HOW LATE] late. [ANY HISTORY, e.g. "They have always
paid on time before."]

Task: write a polite reminder that mentions the invoice date and asks for
payment by [DEADLINE]. Don't threaten, don't offer any discount.

Format: about six lines, a clear subject line, signed [SIGN-OFF].
```

**Notes:** check that it didn't add a late fee or a discount. If it sounds stiff, follow up with "Make it sound like a person, not a bank."

## Test inputs (fictional, safe to use)

Use these to test your templates, so you never need real data while building your library.

**For an email template**
- A Pune printing shop's client, "Sharma Traders", has not paid invoice INV-0457 dated 5 September, now four weeks late. They have always paid on time before. Ask for payment by Friday.

**For a meeting-notes template**
> Weekly sales huddle, Hyderabad branch. Priya to send the revised price list to all dealers by Wednesday. Arjun to visit the two Secunderabad dealers, date not fixed. Festive season stock order needs approval from head office, Priya to follow up. Next huddle Monday 10 am.

**For a style-matching template (two examples)**
> "Hot pakoras, a little rain and nowhere to be. Our monsoon platter is back till Sunday."
> "Fresh, warm, and gone by noon. Our methi thepla is made every morning at 7."
> New item: a paneer kathi roll, launch offer of a free lassi on weekdays.

**For a step-by-step template**
> Two laptops for a college student who mostly writes reports and joins video classes. Laptop P: ₹48,000, 8 GB memory, 6 hours battery, 1 year warranty. Laptop Q: ₹54,000, 16 GB memory, 10 hours battery, 2 years warranty. Budget: up to ₹55,000.

**For a table template**
> Three customer reviews: "Delivery was quick but the box was damaged." "Loved it, ordering again for my sister." "Okay product, nothing special for the price."
