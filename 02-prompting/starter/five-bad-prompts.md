# Five bad prompts: starter material

Everything here is made up, for practice only. Each section has a **bad prompt** and the **material** you'll need to fix it. Add the material to your chat whenever the lab tells you to.

---

## Prompt 1: The customer reply

**Bad prompt**

```
Write a reply to this customer.
```

**Material: the customer's message**

> Hi, I ordered a 3-seater sofa (order SB-20417) from your Nagpur showroom on 2 October and was promised delivery by 12 October. It's now 22 October and nobody has called me. I took leave from work twice to wait at home. I want to know when it's coming, and I think you should refund the ₹1,500 delivery charge. Anjali Rao

**Facts you know (the customer doesn't yet)**

- You are the customer care team at Shree Balaji Furnishings, a family-run furniture shop.
- The fabric supplier delivered late. The sofa is now ready and can be delivered on Saturday, 25 October, between 10 am and 1 pm.
- The store manager has approved refunding the ₹1,500 delivery charge.
- The usual sign-off is "Warm regards, Customer Care, Shree Balaji Furnishings".

## Prompt 2: The meeting notes

**Bad prompt**

```
Summarise these meeting notes.
```

**Material: raw notes from a planning meeting**

> Annual Day planning, Greenfield Public School, Bengaluru. Present: Mrs Iyer (principal), Mr D'Souza (sports), Ms Kaur (music), Ravi (admin), Fatima (parent rep). Date fixed for 20 December, assembly hall, 5 pm. Ms Kaur will finalise the songs list and share with class teachers by next Friday. Need sound system quote, Ravi to get 3 quotes, no date given. Fatima says parents want a food stall, PTA can run it, Fatima to confirm volunteers by 1 December. Mr D'Souza wants a 10-minute drill display, needs ground practice slots, will ask Mrs Iyer, Mrs Iyer said okay in principle but timetable clash with exams on 8 and 9 December so no practice those days. Chief guest still not decided, Mrs Iyer to shortlist two names. Budget discussed, last year ₹1.8 lakh, this year probably similar, Ravi to check accounts. Printing invitations: who? Not decided. Next meeting 14 November.

## Prompt 3: The Instagram caption

**Bad prompt**

```
Write an Instagram caption for our new filter coffee.
```

**Material: two past captions that did well**

> **Caption for our masala chai (liked by 1,240 people):**
> Monsoon outside. Ginger, cardamom and a little too much sugar inside. Just how Amma made it. Pull up a chair, the second cup is on us till Sunday. #KaapiCorner #Coimbatore

> **Caption for our banana bread (liked by 980 people):**
> We tried to make it healthy. Then we added jaggery and walnuts, and nobody complained. Fresh out of the oven every morning at 8. #KaapiCorner #BakedToday

**About the new product**

- Kaapi Corner is a small café in Coimbatore.
- New: traditional filter coffee, made with a decoction from a local coffee estate, served in a steel tumbler and dabarah.
- Launch offer: free tumbler refill on weekdays before 10 am, this month only.

## Prompt 4: The cheaper plan

**Bad prompt**

```
Which plan is cheaper?
```

**Material: two co-working offers for a team of 6 people, for 12 months**

> **Plan A, WorkNest:** ₹7,500 per seat per month. If you sign for 12 months, you get 1 month free (you pay for 11 months). No setup fee.

> **Plan B, DeskHub:** ₹38,000 per month, flat, for up to 8 seats. One-time setup and security deposit handling fee of ₹25,000 (not refundable).
