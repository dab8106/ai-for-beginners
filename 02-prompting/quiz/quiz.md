# Quiz: Prompting that works

10 questions. Try each one before you look at the answer: the correct option is in **bold**, and the short explanation under it says why. Aim for 7 out of 10. If you miss one, rewatch the lecture named next to it.

**1. What are the four parts of a good prompt?** _([Lecture 8](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ypvup6hwrPk))_

- **Role, context, task and format**
- Greeting, question, thanks and sign-off
- Topic, length, language and tone
- Keywords, hashtags, links and emojis

> Give the AI a role, the context, a clear task, and the format you want back.

**2. A colleague types 'summarise this meeting'. The summary is long and the action items are buried. Which part was missing?** _([Lecture 8](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ypvup6hwrPk))_

- The role
- The greeting
- **The format**
- Nothing: the AI just made a mistake

> Ask for the shape you need: a short summary, then action items with an owner for each.

**3. What's a common prompting mistake?** _([Lecture 8](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ypvup6hwrPk))_

- Giving the AI a role
- **Adding more words instead of the right words**
- Asking for a format
- Using the same prompt in two assistants

> A long prompt with no context is still a weak prompt. The right details matter more than length.

**4. You want product descriptions in your shop's own style. What works best?** _([Lecture 9](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UKncKUWVZLE))_

- Describe the style with adjectives like 'catchy' and 'friendly'
- Ask for twenty versions
- Ask for the description in capital letters
- **Include two or three of your best past descriptions as examples**

> AI picks up a style far better from examples than from adjectives. This is few-shot prompting.

**5. Every example in your prompt is about a red sari. What might go wrong?** _([Lecture 9](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UKncKUWVZLE))_

- Nothing: more alike is better
- **The AI may mention red in the new description too**
- The AI will refuse to answer
- The prompt becomes zero-shot

> Use varied examples, so the AI learns the pattern, not one product.

**6. Which task benefits most from asking the AI to work step by step?** _([Lecture 10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qoEMprBgf8c))_

- **Choosing which of three laptops fits your budget and needs**
- Translating a thank-you note
- Fixing a spelling mistake
- Making a sentence shorter

> Step by step helps with calculations, comparisons and decisions, and the visible steps let you check the logic.

**7. You want 20 customer reviews labelled positive, negative or neutral, to count them in a spreadsheet. What do you ask for?** _([Lecture 11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CauvI8IM91o))_

- A paragraph for each review
- A poem about the reviews
- **A table with one row per review and a column for the label**
- A single overall score

> Rows are easy to count and filter. Paragraphs are not.

**8. When asking for a table of action items, why add 'If there's no deadline, write not set'?** _([Lecture 11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CauvI8IM91o))_

- It makes the table shorter
- It's required for JSON
- Spreadsheets can't read empty cells
- **Otherwise the AI may fill the gap and invent a date**

> Always say what to write when information is missing, so nothing gets invented.

**9. Your product description sounds too salesy. Which follow-up works better?** _([Lecture 12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d2Ioa-Hk72E))_

- Make it better
- **Remove the hype words, and keep it under fifty words**
- Try again
- Write it like a professional

> Specific feedback names the problem and the target, so the AI doesn't have to guess.

**10. After a few rounds of feedback, you finally get the answer you wanted. What should you do next?** _([Lecture 12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d2Ioa-Hk72E))_

- Nothing: the chat is saved anyway
- Delete the chat
- **Fold your feedback back into the original prompt, and save it to your prompt library**
- Send the same feedback again

> Updating the original prompt means it works in one go next time. That's what makes a prompt library useful.
