> Think of it as a password for your app. Keep it private, and pair it with a spending limit.

**7. You're unsure whether to give some information to an AI tool. What's the simple test from the lesson?** _([Lecture 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gURuQVXo5rw))_

- **Would I post this in a public group? If not, don't send it**
- Is it shorter than one page?
- Is it in English?

> If you wouldn't post it publicly, don't give it to an AI tool. Swap real names and numbers for made-up ones.
