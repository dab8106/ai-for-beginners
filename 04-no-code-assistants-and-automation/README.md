## Think about it

[Lecture 21: No-Code vs Code for AI: When to Switch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pczZlc9RsG4) asks you to open the email automation from the lab [Email to AI summary to Google Sheet](labs/email-to-sheet.md) and ask: what would break at ten times the volume? Write down your answers for cost (one paid AI call per email), speed, errors (what happens to an email the AI step can't label), privacy (every email goes to the AI provider), and who checks the results. Those answers tell you when it's time to move from no-code to code.
