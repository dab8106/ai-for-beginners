# Deccan Desk Co-working: Pricing Sheet

*Fictional company and fictional prices, made up for training only.*

Valid from 1 April 2026 until further notice. All prices are in Indian rupees (INR) and **exclude 18% GST**, which is added to every invoice.

## Deposits and discounts

- **Security deposit:** one month's plan price, for Dedicated Desk and Private Cabin plans only. Refundable.
- **Annual prepayment:** pay 12 months in advance and get 10% off the plan price. Not valid on Day Pass or add-ons.
- **Student discount:** 20% off Hot Desk for full-time students with a valid college ID.
- Discounts cannot be combined.
