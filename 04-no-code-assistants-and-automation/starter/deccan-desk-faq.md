# Deccan Desk Co-working: Member FAQ

*Fictional company, made up for training only.*

Deccan Desk is a co-working space on the 3rd and 4th floors of Shreyas Tower, Baner Road, Baner, Pune 411045. Last updated: 1 October 2026.

## Opening hours

- **Front desk:** Monday to Saturday, 9:00 am to 8:00 pm. Closed on Sundays and on the public holidays listed on the notice board.
- **Building access:** Day pass and Flexi members can use the space during front desk hours only. Dedicated Desk and Private Cabin members get a key card with 24x7 access.

## Wi-Fi and printing

- There are two networks: **DeccanDesk-Members** (fast, for members) and **DeccanDesk-Guest** (slower, for visitors).
- The member Wi-Fi password is printed on the card at the front desk and changes on the 1st of every month. Staff will not share it by phone, email or chat.
- Every member gets 100 black-and-white printed pages per month. Colour printing and extra pages are billed at the rates in the pricing sheet.

## Meeting rooms

- There are three meeting rooms: **Sinhagad** (4 seats), **Rajgad** (8 seats) and **Shaniwar Wada** (14 seats, with a video-conference screen).
- Book rooms in the Deccan Desk member app. Bookings are in 30-minute slots.
- Monthly members get free meeting room credits each month (see the pricing sheet). Unused credits do not carry over to the next month.
- If you cancel a booking less than 2 hours before it starts, the credits or charges are not refunded.

## Parking

- Two-wheeler parking in the basement is free for all members.
- Four-wheeler parking is limited. Dedicated Desk and Private Cabin members can rent a reserved car slot by the month. There is no visitor car parking; visitors can use the paid public parking at Baner Pashan Link Road.

## Guests and visitors

- Members can bring up to 2 guests per day, free of charge, for up to 2 hours.
- Every guest must sign in at the front desk and show a photo ID.
- Guests staying longer than 2 hours need a day pass.

## Mail and courier

- Monthly members can receive letters and courier parcels at the Deccan Desk address. We hold parcels at the front desk for 7 days.
- Using Deccan Desk as your **registered business address** (for GST or company registration) is a separate add-on. See the pricing sheet.

## Food and drinks

- Tea, coffee and filtered water are free all day in the pantry on each floor.
- The ground-floor cafe, **Chai Point Baner** (an independent business), gives members a 10% discount on showing the member app.

## Billing and cancellations

- Monthly plans are billed in advance on the 1st of each month.
- To cancel a monthly plan, give **30 days' notice** by email to billing@deccandesk.example.
- The security deposit is refunded within 15 working days after you return your key card.
