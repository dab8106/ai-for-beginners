# Deccan Desk Co-working: House Rules

*Fictional company, made up for training only.*

These rules keep Deccan Desk a calm, safe place to work. Repeated breaches can lead to a written warning and then to cancellation of membership.

1. **Quiet zone.** The 4th floor is a quiet zone. No phone or video calls at your desk there. Use the phone booths or a meeting room.
2. **Calls on the 3rd floor.** Short calls at your desk are fine, at a normal speaking volume. Use headphones for video calls.
3. **Phone booths.** There are four single-person phone booths. Please don't use one for more than 45 minutes at a time if someone is waiting.
4. **Food.** Hot meals only in the pantry or the terrace. Snacks and drinks are fine at your desk. Please clean up after yourself.
5. **Clean desk.** Open desks are shared. Take your things with you at the end of the day. Items left overnight go to the front desk.
6. **Lost and found.** Items are kept at the front desk for 30 days, then donated.
7. **No smoking or vaping** anywhere in the building, including the terrace and the stairs.
8. **Pets.** No pets, except trained assistance animals.
9. **After hours (24x7 members).** Between 8:00 pm and 9:00 am, sign the after-hours register at the entrance and make sure the main door closes behind you. Don't let anyone in who doesn't have their own key card.
10. **Guests.** You are responsible for your guests. They must sign in at the front desk and stay with you.
11. **Events.** Members can host events in Shaniwar Wada after 6:00 pm with 7 days' notice and the community manager's approval.
12. **Safety.** Fire exits must stay clear. In an emergency, follow the floor marshal and gather at the assembly point in the front parking area.
13. **Respect.** No harassment, discrimination or selling to other members without their consent. Report any concern to the community manager.
